# Code of conduct

ndekoui follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work, and leave room for people who are new. Harassment of any kind is not accepted.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through [@ndekocode](https://x.com/ndekocode). Reports are read by the maintainer only and answered within a few days.
